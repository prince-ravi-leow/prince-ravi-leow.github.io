I work on computer vision for animal behaviour and health. My recent work links poultry behaviour from video to health and welfare. The aim is an objective, early warning of disease and poor welfare from nothing more than a camera.

### Deep learning from videography as a tool for measuring *E. coli* infection in poultry

Published at Royal Society Open Science, 2025, co-author

* [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.250151)

We link *E. coli* infection in broiler chickens to changes in their behaviour using video footage. My talk on this work won [Best Lightning Talk at Health Data Science Day 2025](https://lnkd.in/p/exNUscXH).
